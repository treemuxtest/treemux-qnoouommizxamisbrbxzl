Neon Drift Pulse is a one-screen arcade toy that starts instantly: you move, collide, explode, and score within seconds of page load. The core mechanic is razor-simple and addictive: steer a glowing orb to harvest sparks while weaving through hunter drones, then detonate a pulse at just the right moment to convert danger into points. Every contact is juiced with particle bursts, screen shake, reactive neon backgrounds, and punchy synthesized sound so the game feels crisp even in short sessions. It is fully client-side, hardcoded, and self-contained in a single Next.js page, proving how much depth and polish can come from one mechanic tuned for pure game feel.
