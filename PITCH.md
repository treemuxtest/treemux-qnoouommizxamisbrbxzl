Pulse Garden is a zero-friction browser toy where your cursor becomes a magnetic brush that sculpts a living particle ecosystem the instant the page loads. Clicks trigger shockwave rings, hold-and-drag paints momentum into the field, and quick keyboard toggles flip the world between attraction and repulsion so tiny choices cause dramatic chain reactions. Every visual and sound is fully procedural, so there are no assets, no loading state, and no backend: just pure emergent play in a single page. It feels less like a game menu and more like discovering a new physical material you can improvise with.
